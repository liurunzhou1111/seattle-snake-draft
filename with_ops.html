Methodology

Performance score = 100 × Σ(Pearson-derived weight × player Min-Max normalized feature). Cost efficiency = score ÷ salary in $M. The optimization maximizes total performance score, not the score-to-salary ratio.

Salary and roster limits are hard constraints. All scores are relative indices, not predicted games won. A scenario is recalculated live from the six feature weights.
